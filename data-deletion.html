Wavro — Data Deletion

Last updated: September 30, 2026

This page explains how to delete your Wavro account and the data associated with it.

Using Wavro without an account

You can seal capsules and get matches without an account. In that case the only data stored for you is a random token on your device. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app removes it — there is nothing tied to your identity to delete.

Deleting your account and data

If you created an account (email sign-up), you can request full deletion of your account and associated data at any time. To do so, email us at developer.dksk@gmail.com from the email address associated with your Wavro account, with the subject line "Delete my account".

We will process your request and confirm by email. Requests are normally completed within 30 days.

What gets deleted

When your account is deleted, we remove:

  • Your account and email address.
  • Your profile (alias, visibility settings, and your 18+ confirmation flag).
  • Your capsules (mood, genres, and the songs/artists you listed).
  • Lists you own, and your membership and contributions in shared lists.
  • Your social connections (who you follow and who follows you).

What may remain

Aggregated, anonymized analytics that cannot identify you (for example, overall usage counts) may be retained. We may also keep limited records where required to comply with legal obligations or to prevent abuse; these are deleted once no longer needed.

Questions

For any question about deleting your data, contact developer.dksk@gmail.com.
