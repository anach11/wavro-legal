Wavro — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 30, 2026

Wavro ("we", "us") is a music-discovery app that connects people with similar taste. This policy explains what we collect, why, and your choices. By using Wavro you agree to this policy.

1. Anonymous use vs. accounts

You can seal capsules and get matches without an account. In that case we store only a random token on your device to power matching — no email, no name.

Social features (following people, shared lists, public profiles) require an account and are for users aged 18 or older. Age is self-declared at sign-up: we ask your date of birth to check you are 18+, but we do not store your date of birth — we store only a yes/no flag that you confirmed you are of age.

2. What we collect

  • Account information — your email address, provided when you create an account with email and a password.
  • Profile — your alias, whether your alias is shown, whether your profile is public or private, and your 18+ confirmation flag.
  • Content you create — your capsules (mood, genres, songs and artists you list), and lists you create or join (list names, songs added, and which member added each one).
  • Social connections — who you follow and which lists you belong to.
  • Anonymous token — a random identifier stored on your device for anonymous matching.
  • Approximate region — we infer a broad region (e.g., continent) from your device's time zone to improve matching. We do not collect GPS or precise location, and we do not request location permissions.
  • Usage and device data — via Firebase Analytics (app events and device/app identifiers) to understand how the app is used.
  • Advertising data — Google AdMob may collect an advertising identifier and device information to serve ads.

3. How we use your information

  • To match you with people who share your taste and to power the social features (follows, lists, profiles).
  • To keep Wavro safe: automated filtering of prohibited words, and user reporting and blocking.
  • To understand usage (analytics) and to show ads.

4. Content visibility and other users

When your profile is public, your sealed capsules are visible to your followers. Each capsule can also be marked private individually. In a public list, anyone with the list code can see the list and the songs in it, including which member added each song. You control what you make public.

5. Moderation and safety

List names and songs added to public lists are checked against a list of prohibited terms on our servers. Users can report content and block other users. We may remove content or accounts that violate our rules.

6. Who we share data with

We do not sell your personal data. We use the following service providers:

  • Supabase — backend, authentication and database (stores your account and content).
  • Google — Firebase Analytics (usage analytics) and AdMob (advertising).

Wavro does not play audio. When you open a track, we send you to Spotify, YouTube Music or Apple Music via a link; we do not share your personal data with them beyond opening a search.

7. Data retention and deletion

We keep your data while your account is active. You can request deletion of your account and associated data at any time — see our Data Deletion page. Deleting your account removes your profile, capsules, lists, and social connections.

8. Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Access to your data is restricted by row-level security rules so that users can only read and write what they are permitted to.

9. Children

Wavro's social features are not directed to children and require users to be 18 or older. The anonymous, non-social part of the app is not intended to collect any personal information from children.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. Material changes will be reflected by updating the date above.

11. Contact

For privacy questions or data requests, contact us at developer.dksk@gmail.com.
